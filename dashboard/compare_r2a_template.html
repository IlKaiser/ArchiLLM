1 · Paper vs released tables

16 configurations reported in Table V

The paper's Table V and the R2ABench release are not the same numbers. Syntax validity (SV) and the five L2 judge scores agree; the L1 structural metrics do not — the release re-scored L1 with a newer correspondence version (l1-correspondence-v2), roughly doubling Edge F1 and raising GenPrec and layer accuracy. Paper L1 numbers therefore cannot be reproduced from the release, and only SV and L2 are stable across the two.

2 · Syntax validity on the paper's scale

L0: renders + parses — same rule as the paper

SV is the one metric measured identically here and in the paper (a view counts only if it renders on PlantUML and parses into a graph), so ARCHI and the plain prompt are placed directly among the paper's rows. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash rows come from the release (it is not in the paper). L1/L2 not on this scale placing ARCHI on the paper's L1/L2 needs R2ABench's own scoring scripts (figshare package), which are not in the local release.

3 · ARCHI on R2ABench's own scale

R2ABench evaluation_script, unchanged — gpt-5.4-mini, PlantUML 1.2026.2

Scored with R2ABench's released evaluator: L0 renderer gate, L1 l1-correspondence-v2 alignment + the hierarchy-aware edge step behind Results/l1.csv, the frozen L2 rubric and curated ReqCov, all judged by gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17 at temperature 0. The L1 driver reproduces all 1,128 released L1 rows exactly. SV, L2 and ReqCov are comparable with both the paper and the release; L1 is comparable with the release rows (the paper's Table V L1 predates the release's re-scoring, see section 1). control R2A Direct (re-judged) re-scores R2ABench's own DeepSeek V4.1 Flash direct views here; its gap to the released DeepSeek V4.1 Flash · Direct row is the run-to-run judge variance — differences smaller than that are not meaningful.
